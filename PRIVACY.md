## 1. Introduction & Overview
Laxico Advertising ("we", "our", or "us") respects your personal data and is committed to maintaining transparent, secure, and compliant data practices. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, process, and protect your information when you access our website, use our media discovery platform, request billboard proposals, or interact with our support teams.

We comply with applicable data protection legislation, including the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)** of India and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Directly Provide
When you submit an outdoor media inquiry, request a proposal, or book advertising sites, we may collect:
- **Contact Details:** Full name, business email address, WhatsApp/mobile phone number.
- **Organization Details:** Company/agency name, designation, registered business address, GSTIN (for B2B billing).
- **Campaign Specifications:** Target advertising cities, preferred media formats (e.g., Unipoles, DOOH, Bus Shelters, Metro wraps), campaign flight dates, estimated budget range, and creative artwork assets.

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- **Device & Usage Data:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, pages visited, referral URLs, and session duration.
- **Location Estimates:** Coarse geographical location based on IP address to recommend relevant regional media corridors (e.g., Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad).

### C. Campaign Proof-of-Performance Data
- Live photographs, geo-coordinates, and timestamps collected by our field patrol teams to verify billboard mounting and illumination. These images do not intentionally capture personally identifiable public individuals.

## 3. Purpose & Legal Basis for Data Processing
We collect and use your data solely for legitimate business operations:
1. **Generating Media Proposals:** To formulate customized site inventory availability, estimated daily footfall figures, and rate cards for your requested advertising locations.
2. **Campaign Execution & Communication:** To coordinate artwork proofs, dispatch installation confirmation reports, and provide weekly geo-tagged proof-of-performance photos via WhatsApp or email.
3. **Billing & Statutory Compliance:** To generate tax-compliant GST invoices (`29CTIPS2521P1ZZ`), verify payments, and fulfill audit requirements under Indian corporate and tax laws.
4. **Platform Security & Reliability:** To monitor site performance, prevent unauthorized administrative access, and ensure high availability.

---

## 4. How We Protect Your Information
- **Encryption & Transmission:** Data transmitted via our web forms is secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption).
- **Access Control:** Client contact information and campaign inquiries are restricted to authorized campaign managers and account strategists.
- **Database Integrity:** Secure, isolated storage utilizing industry-standard database security protocols (PostgreSQL / Supabase with Row Level Security and encrypted backups).
- **Administrative Protection:** Dedicated role-based access control with time-limited cryptographic session tokens for the Laxico Control Tower CMS.

---

## 5. Sharing of Information
We strictly **do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data** to third-party brokers or advertisers. Data is shared exclusively under the following strict conditions:
- **Service Partners & Field Teams:** Certified local mounting, printing, and fabrication contractors strictly for the physical execution of your campaign.
- **Government & Civic Authorities:** When mandated by law, court order, or municipal advertising regulatory bodies (e.g., BBMP, MCD, DMRC, Police) for site display permits and statutory compliance.
- **Transactional Service Providers:** Secure email notification services and trusted cloud infrastructure providers bound by non-disclosure and strict data processing agreements.

---

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage
- **Essential Cookies / Storage:** We utilize browser `localStorage` and functional session tokens strictly necessary for site navigation, user preferences, and secure administrative authentication.
- **Analytics:** Minimal, aggregated website traffic telemetry used solely to optimize loading speed and user experience without invasive third-party cross-site profiling.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Your Rights
We retain client information only for as long as required to fulfill active campaigns, provide ongoing media advisory, and satisfy statutory tax record-keeping requirements (typically 7 financial years under Indian Income Tax Act).

Under applicable data protection laws, you retain the rights to:
- **Access & Review:** Request a copy of the personal contact details we hold regarding your account.
- **Correction:** Request rectification of inaccurate or outdated business details or GST records.
- **Withdrawal of Consent / Deletion:** Request the deletion of marketing contact details when there are no active campaigns or outstanding financial obligations.
